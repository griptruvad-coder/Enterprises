FAQ

Good to know.

Who can apply to pitch?

Founders at any stage, from idea to growth. Student founders and first-time entrepreneurs are welcome. Shortlisted startups are contacted directly by the organising team.

Is there a fee to apply for a pitch slot?

Applying is free. Shortlisted founders attend the summit with a valid pass; the Founder Pass is built for them.

Where exactly is the summit?

The Auditorium at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi. The nearest metro station is Supreme Court on the Blue Line.

What are the timings?

22 December 2026, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Arrive by 8:30 AM for registration and entry.

How do I become a sponsor or partner?

Call or email the event organisers listed below. They will share the sponsorship deck and tailor a package for your brand.
